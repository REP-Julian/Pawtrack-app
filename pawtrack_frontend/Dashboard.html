How PawTrack Works

Everything you need to know about adoption, pet registration, matchmaking, and care.

1

Browse in Real Time

Head to Meet the Pets. Available companions are synced directly via Appwrite Realtime. Inspect personality traits, health records, and caregiver notes.

2

Submit Digital Application

Click Adopt Now to fill out the digital adoption form. No page reloads needed — your application is saved to the secure database instantly.

3

Caregiver Review & Home Check

Track your status in My Applications (Under Review → Home Check). Chat directly with the caregiver via the Messages tab.

4

Approved & Welcome Home!

You will receive an instant real-time notification toast when approved. Finalize handover arrangements and welcome your new best friend! 🐾

Private Roster

Keep your personal companions in your private roster. Track vaccination schedules, book vet check-ups, and enter them into the Pet Matchmaker.

Public Adoption Board

If you are fostering or rehoming, check "List for Public Adoption". Your pet appears live to everyone on the adoption board immediately.

PawTrack Cloud Storage

Upload photos with drag-and-drop. Images are saved securely to PawTrack Appwrite Storage with zero quality loss.

Recycle Bin & Safe Restore

Accidentally archived a pet? Go to Profile > Recycle Bin to restore them back to the active board with one click.

How Match Maker Works

Select one of your registered private pets, and our matching system helps find verified playmates and breeding partners based on breed compatibility, age, and location radius.

Select your pet from the left roster
Adjust max distance & breed preferences in Settings
Give a Treat to match or pass to view the next candidate
Start a direct conversation once matched!

How does real-time adoption sync work?

PawTrack connects to Appwrite WebSockets. When any user on any device registers a pet or changes an application status, your screen updates instantaneously without page reloads, accompanied by a warm notification toast.

How do I book a vet visit?

Click Vet Appointments on the sidebar, select your preferred practitioner and clinic, pick an available time, and submit. The appointment appears on your upcoming schedule.

Can I replay the guided tour?

Yes! Click the "Guide & Tour" button at the top of your screen anytime to restart the interactive spotlight tour.
